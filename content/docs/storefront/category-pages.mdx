title: Category pages
description: PULSE orders category and landing-page grids for whoever arrived, so a campaign that sends a thousand people to one page shows each of them the products they came for.

---

A campaign sends a thousand people to one category page, and all of them see the same grid. The
shopper who came from a price-comparison link and the one who came from a gift guide want
different products first. PULSE orders the grid against what each visit implies, with a held-back
share of visits shown your default order, so the difference can be measured.

**Who it is for:** merchandisers running campaigns and landing pages, and any category with more
products than fit above the fold.

## The call

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.thepulseapi.com/api/v1/catalog/ranked" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "x-api-key: pk_live_..." \
  -d '{
    "sessionId": "sess_abc123",
    "organizationId": "org_abc123",
    "categorySlug": "helmets-safety",
    "page": 1,
    "limit": 24
  }'
```

- `categorySlug` takes your own slug or a parent one; leave it out to rank the whole catalogue.
- The whole category is ranked and `limit`, up to 200, only decides how much comes back, so page
  two continues the same order.
- Pass `signals` on the same call to record what the visitor just did before ranking.

With the SDK: `await pulse.rankedCatalog({ limit: 24 })`. Reference:
[`POST /catalog/ranked`](/api/v1/ranking#post-catalogranked).

**Counts toward usage:** yes, one request per page.
