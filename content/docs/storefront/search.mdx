Two shoppers type "headphones". One is buying a gift under a budget, the other wants noise
cancelling for travel. Word matching shows them the same list. PULSE Search ranks each list for the
visit it belongs to, and **reads the rest of the query** too: a price limit, who it is for, the
occasion, whether it is urgent.

**Who it is for:** any storefront with a search box, whether you keep your current engine or not.

## Three ways in

| You have | Call | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A search engine you are keeping | `POST /rerank` | Reorders the results it already returned. The lightest way in |
| Products in hand, no engine | `POST /search` | Ranks the products you pass, against the session |
| A search box | `POST /suggest` | Query suggestions scored on the session. **Free**, since it fires on every keystroke |

```ts
const { results } = await pulse.search(products, { query: "tai nghe cho mẹ dưới 1tr5", limit: 24 });

results[0].scoreBreakdown; // why this product came first, factor by factor
```

## Queries in the shopper's own words

`tai nghe cho mẹ dưới 1tr5` is read as headphones, for a mother, at most 1,500,000.
`ซื้อนาฬิกาให้พ่อ งบ 5 พัน` as a watch for a father, at most 5,000. Prices in `500k`, `2 triệu`,
`S$150`, `RM2,000`, `₱2,500`, `750rb` and `2 juta` are understood as typed. More in
[Queries in the shopper's own words](/api/v1/ranking#queries-in-the-shoppers-own-words).

## What you get back

Every result carries its **score breakdown**: fit to the session, quality, text match, price, and,
when you send return history, how often it stays bought. [What a score is made of](/api/v1/ranking#what-a-score-is-made-of).

**Counts toward usage:** yes for `/search` and `/rerank`; `/suggest` is free.
