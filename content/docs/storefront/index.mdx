A thousand shoppers land on the same page and see the same grid, though they came for different
things. The storefront products read what the current visit implies, from the pages viewed, the
filters set and the words searched, and order your products for it. **No login, no cookies, no
personal data**, and every score comes with the factors that produced it.

| Product | What it ranks | Calls | Counts toward usage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Decision API](/storefront/decision-api) | The live session itself: what this visit is shopping for | `POST /track`, `POST /session` | Yes |
| [Search](/storefront/search) | Search results, from your engine or ours | `POST /search`, `POST /rerank`, `POST /suggest` | Yes, `/suggest` free |
| [Recommendations](/storefront/recommendations) | A recommendation strip, on a first visit too | `POST /catalog/ranked` with `recommendCount` | Yes |
| [Category pages](/storefront/category-pages) | Category and landing-page grids | `POST /catalog/ranked` with `categorySlug` | Yes |

Measuring is free: `POST /event` and `POST /exposure` record what was shown and what happened, so
you can prove the ranking worked, and they never count toward usage.

## Built to run beside what you have

- **It falls back to your own order.** If PULSE is slow or unreachable, the shopper sees the order
  you would have shown anyway.
- **A share of visits is always held back**, ranked without PULSE, so the difference can be
  measured rather than asserted.
- **It reads Southeast Asian searches** in Vietnamese, Thai, Bahasa and English, prices included.

Get the catalogue right first: ranking can only surface facts your catalogue records. The
[catalogue products](/catalogue) find and fix the gaps.
