You have run an audit, repaired something, and possibly wired up the runtime. This page is the
end of the path: what to check before it goes in front of shoppers, and what to expect
afterwards.

## What should I check before shipping?

<Steps>

<Step>

### Keys are the right kind

A publishable key (`pk_live_...`) is designed to be visible in page source. A secret key is not,
and belongs only in a server guide. If a secret key reached a theme file or a client bundle,
revoke it now rather than after the deploy.

</Step>

<Step>

### A held-back group exists

Decide what fraction of traffic never sees PULSE ranking, and keep it. Without a control arm you
cannot tell a good quarter from a good ranker, and you will not be able to answer the first
question your finance team asks.

</Step>

<Step>

### Your own order is the fallback

If the engine is slow or unavailable, the page should render your existing order rather than an
empty shelf. Check it by pointing `endpoint` at a port nothing is listening on and loading the
page.

</Step>

<Step>

### `init()` failure is visible to you

`init()` sends the automatic signals exactly once and swallows its own error. A session whose
`init()` failed stays thin for its whole life, and nothing in the page tells you. Log the
failure, or call
[`captureAutoSignals()`](/sdk#beyond-the-client) again on a later page load.

</Step>

<Step>

### Exposure is recorded

If you rank, call `exposure()` with what was actually rendered. A ranking you cannot tie to what
the shopper saw cannot be evaluated later, and the buffered call costs almost nothing.

</Step>

</Steps>

## What should I watch in the first week?

| Watch | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Sessions with zero signals | Almost always `init()` failing silently, or a key scoped to the wrong origin |
| Abstention rate | The engine declining to rank is working as designed. A sudden move in it usually means the catalogue changed, not the engine |
| Audit readiness, re-run | Catalogues decay. New products arrive unrecorded, and a shelf that scored well last quarter quietly stops |
| Fallback rate | How often your own order was served instead. Should be near zero and is not always |

## What will you not claim for me?

Worth reading before you repeat anything from this site internally.

<Callout type="warn" title="No uplift number">
  We have run no live revenue experiment. We will not quote a conversion lift, a return-rate
  reduction or an ROI multiple, and any figure you see elsewhere attached to those words did not
  come from us.
</Callout>

What we can show is a property of your catalogue: which requirements it can and cannot resolve,
before and after a repair. That is measured, it is reproducible, and it is not the same thing as
a sale.

Three more limits, stated plainly:

- **A fact that exists only inside a photograph is one we can tell you is missing and cannot yet
  recover for you.** Extracting attributes from product imagery is research we are doing, not a
  feature we ship.
- **Our simulation of a complete catalogue can only use values your shelf already records.**
  Where an attribute's recorded values do not represent its true range, the simulation is wrong
  in a way we can name and cannot yet correct.
- **We have no customers to name.** When that changes we will say so with their permission.
