The [Decision Audit](/catalogue/decision-audit) reads your fields, titles and descriptions. Visual
Evidence adds your **photos** as evidence, and answers three questions per buying requirement.

1. **Does a photo show a fact your data lacks?** A port, a colour or a dimension diagram already in
   the gallery and missing from the field a filter or an AI assistant reads.
2. **Does a photo contradict your data?** The spec says two HDMI ports and the photo shows one.
3. **Which photo should you add?** A fact a photo could show, that buyers depend on, and that no
   photo shows yet.

It also says what **no** photo can show, battery life or a weight, so those are recorded rather than
read off a picture.

## The call

Photo observations go into the same audit, as `visual`: per product and attribute, the value read
off its photos, or `null` when the photos were checked and do not show it.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.thepulseapi.com/api/v1/catalog/audit" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "x-api-key: pk_live_..." \
  -d '{
    "csv": "id,title,colour,ports\nsku-1,Laptop 14,navy,\nsku-2,Laptop 16,black,HDMI",
    "visual": {
      "sku-1": { "colour": "green", "ports": "HDMI, 2 x USB-C" },
      "sku-2": { "ports": null }
    }
  }'
```

Each answer row in `evidence.rows` then carries `photo`:

| `photo` | What it means | Action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `shown` | In a photo, not in the field | `show`: lift it into the field (`found` holds the value) |
| `contradicts` | A photo disagrees with the recorded value | `reconcile` |
| `not-shown` | Recorded, but no photo shows it | `show`: add a photo that does |

## How a model reads the photos

A vision model lists what each photo shows; the requirement engine, not the model, decides what it
means. Every model is scored first on our benchmark, which includes questions no photo can answer,
and a correct "cannot tell" scores higher than a lucky guess. In the first run on public product
data (Amazon Berkeley Objects and Amazon Reviews 2023, 149 questions), two models answered "cannot
tell" 98% of the time when no photo could show the fact. That is public data and two models, not
your catalogue, and the answers are not yet human-checked.

<Callout title="It does not create images">
  Visual Evidence tells you which photo matters and why. Your team or studio takes it, and the next
  audit confirms the photo shows what it should.
</Callout>
