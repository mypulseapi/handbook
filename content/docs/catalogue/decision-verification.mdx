Your team, an agency or an enrichment tool says the gaps are filled. Decision Verification checks.
Send the export from before and after the work, list what was claimed, and get back what actually
changed, attribute by attribute, and which buying requirements the catalogue can now answer.

It needs **no traffic and no A/B test**. The proof is in the data, so it is ready the moment the
new export is.

**Who it is for:** whoever signs off the repair work, and anyone paying for enrichment who wants
to see it delivered.

## What you get back

- **Per attribute**, the share of products a shopper could decide on before and after: what
  `improved`, what regressed, what did not move.
- **Per claimed repair**, how many products it actually gained, and how strong the evidence is.
- **`unverifiedClaims`**: anything claimed that the new export does not bear out.
- **Buying requirements** the catalogue can now answer, and any it newly cannot.
- With hours on each claim, the effort behind the result, and a one-line headline to report.

## The call

```bash
curl -X POST "https://api.thepulseapi.com/api/v1/catalog/verify" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -H "x-api-key: pk_live_..." \
  -d '{
    "before": "id,category,size\n1,helmet,\n",
    "after":  "id,category,size\n1,helmet,L\n",
    "repairs": [{ "attribute": "size", "method": "manual", "actualHours": 3 }],
    "category": "Helmets"
  }'
```

Add `organizationId` to keep the result; leave it out and the response says nothing was stored.
Every field: [`POST /catalog/verify`](/api/v1/audit#post-catalogverify).

**Counts toward usage:** yes, one request per verification.
