PULSE infers from **behaviour, not identity**. There is no login to warm up, no purchase history to
accumulate, and no cookie to match against a profile. A first-time anonymous visitor is ranked on
the third click of their first session.

<Callout title="What leaves the browser">
  Signals and a session id. Not names, not emails, not payment data.
</Callout>

## Install

<InstallTabs pkg="@thepulseapi/sdk" />

Or drop it on the page, which is what the storefront integrations use:

```html title="index.html"
<script src="https://cdn.thepulseapi.com/sdk/v0/pulse.iife.js" async></script>
```

The script tag exposes the client as the global `Pulse`, so `Pulse.init(...)` works directly.
