## Reading a page's own history

Every page carries the date it last changed at the foot of the page, beside a link to that one
file's full commit history. That is the quickest way to answer "did this page say something
different last month", which is usually the real question.

The pages are MDX files in
[`content/docs`](https://github.com/mypulseapi/handbook/tree/main/content/docs), so the history is
ordinary git: every revision, who made it, and what else changed in the same commit.
