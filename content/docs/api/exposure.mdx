Ranking cannot be graded without knowing what was actually shown. A click rate over results
nobody saw is not a measurement.

## `POST /exposure`

```ts
pulse.exposure(decisionId, items);   // buffered
await pulse.flushExposures();        // send now
```

`exposure` buffers, because a product grid renders many items at once and one request per card
would be absurd. `flushExposures` sends what is pending, and the SDK also flushes on page hide.

<Callout title="Send what rendered, not what you requested">
  If you asked for 24 and showed 12 above the fold, the honest exposure is what the shopper could
  actually see. Over-reporting exposure makes your own ranking look worse than it is.
</Callout>
